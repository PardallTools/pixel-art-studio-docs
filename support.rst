Support
-------

See the :doc:`faq` and the :doc:`troubleshooting` pages.

If you need further help, check `Pixel Art Studio's forums <https://alfredbaudisch.itch.io/pixel-art-studio/community>`_. You can also report bugs `on GitHub <https://github.com/pardalltools/pixel-art-studio-docs/issues>`_.

.. tip::
    The three buttons at the top right of the ``Pixel Art Studio`` panel in the sidebar open these docs, the forums and the bug reports.
