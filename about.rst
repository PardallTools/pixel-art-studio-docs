Writing behind the add-on
-------------------------

Pixel Art Studio exists because of the vertex color heavy look of Nintendo 64 and early PC games. If you want to read about that research, and about how those games used vertex colors and texture blending:

- `Exploring and making Nintendo 64 graphics <https://alfredbaudisch.com/experiments/3d-art/exploring-making-nintendo-64-graphics-n64/>`_
- `Banjo-Kazooie N64 environments and levels: texture blending and vertex color usage <https://alfredbaudisch.com/experiment-logs/banjo-kazooie-n64-environments-and-levels-texture-blending-and-vertex-color-usage/>`_
